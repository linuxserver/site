We would like to announce that we now have a Discord Server! Please come and join us at: [https://discord.gg/YWrKVTn](https://discord.gg/YWrKVTn)

![](/content/images/2017/09/52a.png)

Over the years we've tried various forms of community building from forums to comment sections to IRC. We'd like to invite you to our new Discord server which is replacing Slack for team comms and maybe, ultimately IRC too - but we'll see about that.

It is our hope that this becomes the new home of the LSIO community and allows us to engage with everyone better. Come one, come all. Docker support, home server questions, etc... All are welcome.

We look forward to seeing you there!
