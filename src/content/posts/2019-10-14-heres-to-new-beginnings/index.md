We have some great news and some not so great, to start off with IronicBadger is pursuing his podcast passion with a new show all about [Self Hosting](https://selfhosted.show/) airing on the Jupiter network . It's something he's been passionate about for a long time (you may have heard his frequent guest spots on Linux Unplugged) and he's really excited that this opportunity has come.

[SelfHosted](https://selfhosted.show/)

> Discover new software and hardware to get the best out of your network, control smart devices, and secure your data on cloud services. Self-Hosted is a chat show between Chris and Alex two long-time "self-hosters" who share their lessons and take you on the journey of their new ones.

Which leaves the not so great. IronicBadger is hanging up his LSIO hat to concentrate on his new projects and ventures. While we are sorry to see him leave and are grateful for his past contributions, we are also excited to see what he produces. We know he'll continue to be a presence in the community — after all, self hosting is what we do — and he has a captive audience in us.

So we just want to say, all the best Alex, have a great time doing what you love, that's all you can hope for in life.
