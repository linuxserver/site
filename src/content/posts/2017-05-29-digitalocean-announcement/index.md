I am delighted to announce a new 'special relationship' between LinuxServer.io and DigitalOcean.

![](/content/images/2017/05/DO_Logo_Horizontal_Blue-3db19536-1.png)

Those wonderful people over at DO have agreed to sponsor LSIO. They're providing us a very generous amount of credit which we will use to host this site and much of our other build infrastructure. If you'd like to sign up for their services you can using the following referral [link](https://m.do.co/c/246bf643f947). If you haven't used their (excellent) infrastructure before, give it a whirl. We were using it anyway even before this sponsorship.

We hope to generate some interesting content in terms of potential projects or insights of how to use a CI pipeline and cluster docker hosts together too.

Anyway, *massive* thanks to DigitalOcean for their generous sponsorship of this site and supporting open source!
